# Support Tickets

Customers can raise a support ticket through their customer portal login. Staff handle it as a third messaging channel alongside WhatsApp and SMS.

**Conversations -> Tickets tab**, or navigate to `/admin/tickets`

---

## What It Is

Where WhatsApp and SMS conversations are started by either side, tickets are always **started by the customer** — from their customer portal account. Once raised, staff reply and manage status from the CRM.

---

## Viewing Tickets

The Tickets tab (inside [Conversations](60-conversations.md)) or the dedicated `/admin/tickets` page lists tickets with:

- Customer name
- Latest message preview
- Status: **Open** or **Closed**

Use the status filter to switch between Open and Closed tickets.

---

## Replying to a Ticket

1. Click a ticket to open it
2. Read the message thread
3. Type your reply and send

The customer sees your reply in their portal in real time.

---

## Closing a Ticket

Once resolved, change the ticket's status to **Closed** from the ticket detail view. Closed tickets remain visible under the Closed filter — nothing is deleted.

---

## Requirements

Support tickets require the customer portal to be enabled for your company, since customers can only raise a ticket by logging into their own portal account. If you don't offer customer portal access yet, this channel won't have any traffic.
